Who is Zeni?

Zeni is a personal AI chatbot built by Arman as a passion project. Designed for natural conversations, quick answers, and everyday help all inside a clean, mobile-first interface.

It started as a small experiment and grew into a fully working AI assistant. Not a commercial product just something useful, built openly for everyone.

Backend
How it Works

Zeni runs on Google's Gemini models accessed via Google AI Studio API. Each agent uses its own dedicated API key so traffic is balanced and limits don't interfere with each other. The entire frontend is custom-built from scratch with no frameworks.

Privacy Notice
Please read before using

Your messages are sent to Google's Gemini API to generate responses. Your chats are stored only in your browser's local storage — we do not collect or see them. However, Google may use conversation data to improve their models. Be careful about what you share.

  • Do not share passwords, OTPs, or login credentials.
  • Do not share Aadhaar, PAN, or any ID numbers.
  • Do not share bank details or financial information.
  • Do not share any sensitive personal data.
  • Use Incognito Mode for sensitive conversations nothing is saved.
